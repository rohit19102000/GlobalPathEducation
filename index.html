About Us

A student-focused education consultancy

Global Path Education helps students explore the right international education opportunities. We help them take confident steps towards their global academic journey.

Our counsellors have 9+ years of experience in education counselling. They have guided students through admissions for undergraduate and postgraduate courses in the UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Australia and New Zealand, along with many more countries across Europe, Asia and beyond.

We do more than recommend universities. We understand each student's academic background, career goals, preferred destination and needs. Then we give personalised, end-to-end support – from course selection and scholarships to your visa and flight.

We believe the right guidance can turn a student's ambition into a well-planned global education journey.
